INSTALL MOSS ON YOUR IPHONE

1. Save Moss-unsigned.ipa to the Files app on your iPhone.
2. In Signulous (app or signulous.com dashboard), upload Moss-unsigned.ipa,
   sign it, then install it. Wait for the Moss leaf icon on your Home Screen.
3. Open Moss. On Wi-Fi, with at least 15 GB free, tap Download. Leave
   "Include Fast adapter" off for the first try. Keep Moss open until it says
   "Model verified · ready offline" (12.1 GB download, then a checksum pass).
4. First image: choose 1:1 (512 x 512) and Best or Balanced, type a short
   prompt, tap Generate, and keep Moss on screen. Expect many minutes per image.

Fast mode: Settings -> Model -> Include Fast adapter -> download (1.2 GB more),
then choose Fast. It uses the separate 8-step distilled adapter.

If Moss closes while generating, reopen it (it explains what happened), close
other apps, let the phone cool, and try 512 x 512 with a short prompt.
Do not uninstall Moss to troubleshoot: that deletes its downloaded model and
gallery.

MEMORY ENTITLEMENTS
The app asks for Apple's "increased memory limit" and "extended virtual
addressing" permissions. An unsigned IPA cannot grant them to itself; only the
signing profile Signulous uses can. Moss does not depend on them: on the macOS
test harness a complete 512 x 512 image peaked at 1.62 GiB of memory.
