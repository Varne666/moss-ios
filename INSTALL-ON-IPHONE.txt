INSTALL MOSS ON YOUR IPHONE

1. Save Moss-unsigned.ipa to the Files app on your iPhone.
2. In Signulous (app or signulous.com dashboard), upload Moss-unsigned.ipa,
   sign it, then install it. Wait for the Moss leaf icon on your Home Screen.
   If it fails, try Moss-unsigned-no-live-activity.ipa (same app without the
   Dynamic Island add-on).
3. Open Moss. On Wi-Fi, with at least 16 GB free, tap Download (13.3 GB with
   the Fast add-on). Keep Moss open until "Model verified · ready offline".
4. Type a prompt and tap Generate (Fast, 1024 x 1024 by default). Allow Live
   Activities if asked. The first image times your iPhone; later countdowns
   start immediately.
5. You can leave Moss: if iOS allows background work it keeps going (slower,
   on the CPU) and the Dynamic Island keeps counting down; otherwise it pauses
   and continues when you come back.

If Moss closes while generating, reopen it (it explains what happened), close
other apps, let the phone cool, and try again or pick a smaller size.
Do not uninstall Moss to troubleshoot: that deletes its downloaded model and
gallery.

MEMORY ENTITLEMENTS
The app asks for Apple's "increased memory limit" and "extended virtual
addressing" permissions. An unsigned IPA cannot grant them to itself; only the
signing profile Signulous uses can. Moss does not depend on them: on the macOS
test harness a complete 1024 x 1024 Fast image peaked at 2.7 GiB of memory.
